About

This is a website for showcasing interesting or unique cards from various card games. Below are some resources if you are interested in learning how to play.

Yu-Gi-Oh!

Yugioh is the card game known for being fast-paced and hard to learn. Games are often ended in only 1 - 2 turns, however those couple of turns are usually packed with interactions and back and forth between opponents. The main complaints in regard to Yugioh are its lack of resource system and subsequent power creep.

Learn to play Yu-Gi-Oh!

Magic: The Gathering

Magic the Gathering (mtg) was the worlds first trading card game back when it released in 1993. Mtg offers a variety of different formats and different ways to play. It is a great game to play with a group and not too hard to teach to those unfamiliar with card games. The main downside of mtg its mana system, missing a land on one of your turns can be game losing and can happen no matter how well you optimize your deck.

Learn to play Magic: The Gathering

Riftbound

Riftbound is the newest cardgame by Riot Games it succeeds the digital only card game, Runeterra, and brings the cards to a physical form. Riftbound takes pieces from many different card games most notably inspired by mtg's commander format. The main downside to Riftbound is its game balance and product availability, both of which are sure to be fixed as it find its footing.

Learn to play RiftBound
